# Fictional project monitoring case

All case records and governance rules are synthetic research materials, not real business records.

Project: Northgate Campus Network Modernization
Snapshot: 2025-06-30
Currency: USD

The Northgate Campus Network Modernization project replaces the aging switching, cabling, and wireless infrastructure across three buildings on a single campus. Scope covers a requirements and site survey phase, design of the core and distribution layers, installation of a fiber backbone and structured cabling, deployment of wireless access points and intermediate distribution frame switches, and integration of network monitoring tooling followed by validated cutover. Work is performed by an internal network engineering team supported by contracted cabling crews. The snapshot reflects the reporting point at which approximately the first third of the baseline effort has been consumed, with design work closed out and physical installation underway. Baseline dates, budgets, and effort reflect the approved plan; actual costs and progress reflect records posted through the snapshot date.

Review scope: Monitor the fictional network project at the supplied snapshot. Compare planned and actual progress and costs, forecast the cost outcome under the stated assumptions, assess budget and resource allocation, and recommend justified cost-control actions. Do not execute changes.
